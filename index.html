Proposed 2027 rollout

January 1–December 31, 2027 · Start with CGI, expand to a second organization after pilot review, and evaluate adoption at year-end.

  1. January 1–31, 2027 · CGI pilot

    Test the scorecard on real use cases

    Assign a CGI pilot lead and reviewers. Assess five representative internal AI use cases, capture time to complete and review, and record confusing questions and evidence gaps. Target by January 31: five assessments with documented routing decisions.

  2. February–March 2027 · Second-organization pilot

    Check whether another team can use it

    After CGI reviews the pilot findings, invite one second organization and confirm its review roles and policy requirements. Target by March 31: five additional assessments and a record of which guidance needs local adaptation.

  3. April–June 2027 · Refinement

    Make assessments more consistent

    Use both pilots to clarify field help, evidence expectations, and reviewer handoffs. Target by June 30: publish a revised onboarding guide and have two reviewers independently score three common cases to identify disagreements.

  4. July–September 2027 · Controlled expansion

    Integrate the process into routine intake

    Train designated reviewers at both organizations, establish escalation and follow-up ownership, and review completion and rerouting data monthly. Target by September 30: 20 cumulative assessments across both organizations.

  5. October–December 2027 · One-year milestone

    Decide whether to scale in 2028

    Target by December 31: 30 cumulative assessments across two organizations, with at least 90% documenting a risk tier, reviewer, and next action. Compare median completion time with the January baseline and record the decision to expand, revise, or pause.

Targets are proposed. Expansion depends on pilot evidence and agreement from both organizations.
